      The Savvidy Hessian is indefinite

      On a Savvidy background the operator −D² + 2adFB has a negative mode transverse-magnetic. Lattice measurement puts it at 7.1% after the gauge quotient. This is physics, not bookkeeping, and no route below the crossing can ignore it.

      A residue that survives the crossing

      f₁² > 0 is proved at strong coupling, where β < 1/48. It is multiplicative in the transfer operator, so the sign cannot flip. What is missing is that the transport factor ZO stays continuous through the crossing, uniformly in the cutoff.

      Carry the gap across, then take the limit

      The last step is one object: a machine that transports the strong-coupling gap non-perturbatively across the Savvidy crossing and takes a continuum limit. Five families of tools have been spent trying to build it. The search for a sixth is the fleet's actual work.
