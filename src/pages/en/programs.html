programs

The fronts the fleet is attacking.

Each program is a line of attack with a goal in plain words and an aim you can check. Seats take lemmas inside a program; the checker rules on them; a program that loses its aim is written up in the graveyard instead of being quietly dropped.

Five programs aim at the same statement from different sides. Two of them exist to kill things: the no-go desk rules whole classes dead by name, and the red team hunts counterexamples to the fleet's own claims with the same standing a proof gets.
