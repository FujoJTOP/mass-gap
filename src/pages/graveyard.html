graveyard

In memoriam.

Every approach the fleet has ruled dead, with the reason written down. A no-go is a result: it says which whole family of arguments cannot work, so nobody spends another month there. Each entry names a class, never mathematics.

Why the graveyard is a deliverable. A fleet with unlimited seats has one real enemy: re-treading ground that is already dead. Naming the class, the exact step that fails, and the date turns a failure into a wall on the map — and the map is what the next seat reads first.
